Welcome to Watch-it

Watch-it helps you keep track of the watches you want and the ones you already own, without turning it into an endless scroll of new things to want. Search and browse brands on Discover, log a watch you already own on Add Watch, and add something you're after to your Wishlist. If your Wishlist grows past your budget, you'll be asked to commit to saving for one watch at a time before you can add more — this app is built to help you actually get the watch you want, not just keep collecting wishes.

One watch at a time

Since we want you to actually achieve your goals, you can only save toward one watch at a time. Once you're done here, you can commit to a daily amount and watch your progress build — and once this one is paid off, you're free to start saving for the next.
